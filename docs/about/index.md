# About The YAML Company

## YAML supports your stack; We support YAML

The YAML Company delivers professional maintenance, enterprise support, and compliance assurance for the critical YAML parsers powering global cloud infrastructure.

---

## Our Mission

Our mission is to elevate YAML from an Open Source infrastructure reliant solely on volunteer maintenance into a professionally stewarded, enterprise-hardened cornerstone of global technology infrastructure. We resolve systemic technical debt across the YAML Family of libraries, enforce modern supply chain security standards, and deliver the rigorous accountability that modern compliance demands. We ensure the standard for programmable infrastructure, the one that keeps the Internet running, is secure, reliable, and sustainable for the long term. 

We are making open source sustainable for the enterprises that rely on it, without compromising the open community values that built it.

---

## Our Core Commitments

To fulfill this mission, we operate across three foundational pillars:

**Professional Stewardship**
: Led by YAML’s co-creator, we take direct ownership and maintainership of the core libraries (including [go-yaml](../projects/go-yaml.md), [PyYAML](../projects/pyyaml.md), [libyaml](../projects/libyaml.md), and [yaml-serde](../projects/yaml-serde.md)) that power modern cloud infrastructure.

**Systemic Modernization**
: Rather than applying isolated bug fixes, we treat ecosystem debt holistically. We systematically align libraries with standard specifications, automate release engineering, and integrate robust security frameworks like SLSA compliance and automated fuzzing.

**Commercial Sustainability**
: Through our YAML Enterprise Sustainability (YES) program, we bridge the gap between enterprise security needs and open-source longevity. We provide organizations with early vulnerability notifications and guaranteed support while generating the predictable revenue required to permanently fund core maintenance.

## What We Do

### Professional Maintenance

We deliver dedicated, professional maintenance for critical YAML projects:

- **[go-yaml](../projects/go-yaml.md)** - Powers Kubernetes, Helm, and Kustomize
- **[pyyaml](../projects/pyyaml.md)** - Foundation of Ansible and Python's YAML ecosystem
- **[libyaml](../projects/libyaml.md)** - Fast C library used by many implementations
- **[yaml-serde](../projects/yaml-serde.md)** - Highly-performant Rust binding and serialization library
- **[YAMLScript](../projects/yamlscript.md)** - Next-generation YAML programming language

### Enterprise Support

Through the [YES Program](../yes/index.md), we provide:

- 48-hour early security notifications
- Professional support from YAML experts
- Strategic influence to help decide what YAML builds next
- Training, audits, and consulting services

### Specification Stewardship

We maintain the YAML specification at [yaml.org](https://yaml.org) and coordinate with the YAML language development team to evolve the standard.

## Our Values

**Sustainability over short-term gains**
: We're building a sustainable model for open source infrastructure maintenance, not seeking quick returns.

**Security as an infrastructure baseline**
: Security is not optional. We enforce modern supply chain compliance (including SLSA and SBOMs) to guarantee enterprise-grade protection for all downstream consumers.

**Community and enterprise collaboration**
: We believe enterprises and open source communities can work together for mutual benefit.

**Coordinated transparency**
: All core fixes are public and open source. To protect our enterprise partners, security vulnerabilities undergo a coordinated 48-hour advance quarantine window before being disclosed publicly.
